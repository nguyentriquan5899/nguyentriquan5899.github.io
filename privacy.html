Privacy,
the short cut.

This is a free app made by Nguyen Tri Quan, offered as is. Here's exactly what it collects when you use it, why, and what you get to decide — in plain words.

The 30-second version

  • The app collects a bit of basic technical and usage info to keep improving.
  • It never tracks your precise location, and it uses no AI to process your data.
  • A few trusted services get only aggregated, anonymized data.
  • Want out? Just uninstall — that stops all collection.

01What the app collects

When you download and use the app, it may gather:

  • Your device's IP address

It does not gather precise information about where your device is, and it does not use Artificial Intelligence to process your data or power any feature. From time to time the app may also ask you for certain personal details — anything you provide is kept and used exactly as this page describes.
